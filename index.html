🌀 Welcome to the Emotion Engine

This is an interactive, multi-disciplinary map of 1,878 emotional states, arranged radially by pleasantness (angle) and intensity (distance).

🧭 Two Ways to Explore

  • Philosophical Exploration: Leave the "English terms only" box unchecked (in Map Filters) to explore the entire global landscape. This reveals over 1,400 untranslatable concepts from world cultures, depth psychology, and mythology. Use the Orbit tool in the sidebar to take a guided celestial tour.
  • "Name It to Tame It" (Grounding Tool): Check the "English terms only" box to clear the map into a practical, standard emotion wheel. Click the ✨ Somatic Wizard in the top toolbar to track your physical body tension and map it to a core human need. It will automatically isolate your exact emotional state.

📚 Sources & Attributions

This tool synthesizes foundational concepts from several major schools of psychological thought:

  • The Positive Lexicography Project (Dr. Tim Lomas & hifisamurai): Provided the vast majority of the 1,400+ cross-cultural, untranslatable, and nuanced positive emotional terms that populate the global view.
  • Atlas of the Heart (Dr. Brené Brown): Provided the definitive semantic mapping and relational definitions for complex English emotions (e.g., distinguishing Shame vs. Guilt, Empathy vs. Sympathy, Belonging vs. Fitting In).
  • Analytical Psychology (Carl Jung): Inspired the constellation nodes mapping deep psychic structures (The Self, The Shadow, Persona) and the archetypes underpinning world mythology.
  • The Original Feeling Wheel (Dr. Gloria Willcox & Dr. Robert Plutchik): Provided the foundational concept of arranging emotions radially by intensity and primary valence sectors, which inspired the geometric layout of this engine.
